Towerlords · Boss Cinematics

The guardian talks to you across five beats — who you are & how you climbed, its world, why it guards (by choice or force), its true feeling toward the Tower (fear / hate / love / regret), and the summit (dread it, covet it, or not know it) before it must stop you. Every line is assembled fresh from the game's real pools — ~3.5 billion combinations per guardian — and anti-repeated. Replay the same guardian; it never says the same thing. Audio is live — the intro is a quiet, somber & angry bed (no beats), chosen from 12 variants; the defeat is the slow, rainy loss score, from 10 variants. Each play picks a different one. (Volume up; audio needs one click.)
